# 🏆 Achievement System - Quick Start Guide

## How to Access Achievements

1. Open your FlashcardApp
2. Tap **Settings** (gear icon in top-left)
3. Scroll to **"ERFOLGE"** section
4. Tap the **🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS** button
5. Browse your achievements!

## What You'll See

### Achievements Screen
- **Trophy icon + completion stats** at the top
- **Progress bar** showing overall achievement percentage
- **Category filters** to browse by type
- **Grid of achievement cards** with:
  - Icon (emoji)
  - Title
  - Tier badge (銅/銀/金/白金/💎)
  - Progress bar (if not unlocked)
  - Description

### Unlock Animation
When you earn an achievement, a beautiful popup appears:
- Japanese text: **実績解除!** (Achievement Unlocked!)
- Glowing badge with sparkles ✨
- Achievement details
- Tier badge with color coding
- Auto-closes after 3 seconds or tap "WEITER"

## How to Earn Achievements

### Easy to Get Right Away
- ✅ **First Blood** - Create your first flashcard (just use Add Card button)
- ✅ **First Steps** - Complete 1 day of studying
- ✅ **Beginner** - Review 10 cards

### Study Consistently
- ✅ **Week Warrior** - Study 7 days in a row
- ✅ **Early Bird** - Study before 8 AM
- ✅ **Night Owl** - Study after 10 PM

### Challenge Yourself
- ✅ **Perfect Round** - Get 100% accuracy in a session
- ✅ **Overachiever** - Complete 2x your daily goal
- ✅ **Speed Demon** - Review 20+ cards in one session

### Long-Term Goals
- ✅ **Grand Master** - Review 5,000 cards total
- ✅ **Legend** - Maintain a 365-day streak
- ✅ **Level 50** - Reach Level 50

## Tips for Achievement Hunters

1. **Check progress regularly** - See how close you are to each achievement
2. **Use category filters** - Focus on specific types
3. **Daily consistency** - Streak achievements are very rewarding
4. **Perfect sessions** - Aim for 100% accuracy for multiple achievements
5. **Study at different times** - Unlock Early Bird AND Night Owl
6. **Create cards** - Don't forget the "First Blood" achievement!

## Color Guide

Each achievement glows with its category color when unlocked:
- 🟠 **Orange** = Streak achievements
- 🔵 **Blue** = Cards achievements  
- 🟢 **Green** = Accuracy achievements
- 🟣 **Purple** = Speed achievements
- 🩷 **Pink** = Dedication achievements
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Mastery achievements
- 🩵 **Cyan** = Special achievements
