# 📸 Camera Scanner - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 How to Use (30 seconds)

### Step 1: Open Scanner
```
Dashboard → Tap "+" button → Tap camera icon 📷 (top-right)
```

### Step 2: Scan Page
```
1. Point camera at vocabulary list
2. Wait for green highlights on text
3. Tap anywhere on highlighted text
```

### Step 3: Import Words
```
Review extracted words → Tap "KARTEN ERSTELLEN" → Done!
```

---

## ✅ Best Results

### Perfect Textbook Format:
```
Sonne - sun
Mond - moon
Apfel - apple
Hund - dog
```

### What Works:
- ✅ Clear printed text
- ✅ Good lighting
- ✅ Hold camera 8-12 inches away
- ✅ Standard fonts

### What Doesn't Work Well:
- ❌ Handwritten notes (hit or miss)
- ❌ Fancy script fonts
- ❌ Very small text
- ❌ Poor lighting

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Scan multiple pages**: Do whole chapter at once
2. **Review before import**: Remove mistakes with X button
3. **Missing translations**: Use AI magic wand after import
4. **Bad scan**: Just tap camera again to rescan
5. **Mixed with manual**: Camera for bulk, manual for individual words

---

## 🐛 Quick Troubleshooting

**Problem**: "Camera not available"  
**Fix**: Your device doesn't support it (iPhone X or older). Use manual entry.

**Problem**: "No words detected"  
**Fix**: Make sure words follow format: `German - English`

**Problem**: "Wrong words extracted"  
**Fix**: Remove them with X button before importing

**Problem**: "Translation missing for some words"  
**Fix**: Normal for single-word lists. Add manually or use AI.
